Project Crown

Support for the fair match-3 puzzle adventure.

Need help?

Open a request in Project Crown Support Issues. Please include your device model, iOS version, level number, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Common questions

  • No legal moves: the board automatically reshuffles for free after animations finish.
  • Restore progress: this release stores progress locally on the device and does not require an account.
  • Purchases: this release contains no real-money purchases or subscriptions. The in-game shop uses Coins earned through play.
  • Hints: Royal Hint highlights one strong move but never plays it automatically.
