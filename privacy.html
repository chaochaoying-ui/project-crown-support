Privacy Policy

Project Crown · Effective August 27, 2026

Overview

Project Crown is designed to be played without an account. This release does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not send gameplay analytics to the developer or third parties.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, stars, earned Coins, booster inventory, settings, and a bounded local gameplay-event history on your device. This information is used only to operate the game and is not transmitted off the device by this release.

Advertising, purchases, and third parties

This release contains no advertising, no tracking SDK, no account system, no social login, no StoreKit purchase, and no subscription. The in-game shop uses only Coins earned through gameplay.

Children's privacy

Because this release does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open a request in Project Crown Support Issues. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
